03 · WHY
With 108 trials over 3,700 sessions, noise alone is expected to produce a maximum Sharpe of 6.5. The strategy showed 0.42. The search cannot tell this edge from luck — because there is no edge.

That single line is the product. Once you account for how many variants were tried, and for the returns' skew (−3.1) and heavy tails, the deflated Sharpe rounds to zero. A number near 1.0 became a number near nothing.
