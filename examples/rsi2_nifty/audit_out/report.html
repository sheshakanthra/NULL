Why it failed

beats_benchmark_netNo alpha over buy-and-hold after costs. Strategy returned 3.16% CAGR net of everything against 13.25% for the benchmark, beta -0.01. Annualised alpha 3.49% with a t-stat of 1.58 (Newey-West, 8 lags) over 3,700 observations; threshold is 2.0, and alpha with a t-stat below 2 is not alpha.

deflated_sharpeThe probability the true Sharpe exceeds zero, after adjusting for how many variants were tried and for the shape of the return distribution, is 0.00 against a threshold of 0.95. Adjusted for selection, this is a search finding noise.

reality_checkBootstrapping the null that the best candidate does not beat the benchmark, using a stationary bootstrap so autocorrelation survives resampling, gives p = 0.977 against a threshold of 0.050. The outperformance is within what chance produces on this history.

capacityThe largest single order would consume 5.1% of 20-day average daily traded value, against a tolerance of 5.0%. An order this size does not execute at the prices the backtest assumed; the returns shown are not attainable at this capital.

selection diagnosticWith 108 trials over 3,700 observations, noise alone is expected to produce a maximum Sharpe of 6.52; the candidate showed 0.42, so the search cannot distinguish this edge from luck.

Evidence panels

These are reported for context and do not vote on the verdict.

drawdownMaximum peak-to-trough decline was 30.4%, with the longest underwater period lasting 1,029 bars. Reported for context; it does not vote. Drawdown answers whether you would hold a strategy, which is a preference about risk appetite, not whether it has an edge. A 35% limit would reject a NIFTY-like buy-and-hold, which draws down around 60% through 2008.
mtrlAn annualised Sharpe of 0.42, with skew -3.08 and kurtosis 69.05, would need 16.6 years of observations before it could be called significant at 95% confidence. The backtest is 14.7 years long, so it is under-powered by construction: this Sharpe would need a longer track record than exists before it could be called significant, and no analysis of the existing data changes that.
pboAcross 12,870 symmetric train/test partitions of 108 variants, the in-sample best landed below the out-of-sample median 21% of the time (median relative rank 0.77). Under no selection skill every candidate is exchangeable and PBO's expected value is exactly 0.5, so the point estimate alone proves nothing; a moving-block subsampling interval over 60 moving-block subsamples of the underlying series gives [0.05, 0.34] at 95% confidence, and the interval lies entirely below the 0.5 null. This is reported as evidence only and does not vote on the verdict. Each replicate used a random 512 of the 12,870 partitions to keep the cost tractable; the point estimate uses all of them. A low PBO is never evidence that an edge is real. With many candidates over a short sample, the maximum of N noise Sharpes routinely exceeds a genuine edge's own Sharpe, so the real strategy is not the one selected and PBO ends up describing a search that never found it.

Stated limitations — all of them

  • blockingCharge rates are taken from the published Indian equity charge stack and have NOT been reconciled against a live broker contract note (configs/costs_india_equity.yaml carries _verified_on: UNVERIFIED). Cost LEVELS on this report -- every rupee figure, every cost-drag percentage, every net Sharpe -- carry that error directly. A RANKING between variants priced under the same rates does not automatically, but whether a particular ranking survives a plausible rate error is a measurable question rather than an assumption. Rate sensitivity WAS measured rather than assumed (examples/rsi2_nifty/cost_robustness.csv: 25 full re-runs, every charge component scaled independently over 0.75x to 1.25x). The claim that the best-by-gross variant is NET-NEGATIVE does NOT survive it: that fails in 4 of 25 cases (stt_buy_pct@0.75, stt_sell_pct@0.75, half_spread_bps@0.75, ALL_COMPONENTS_TOGETHER@0.75), reaching +0.222 at its least negative. It is a sign test on a number close to zero, and this report does not establish it. What did survive every case: across all 25 cases the naive gross pick ranked 24-93 of 108 on the net-ranked grid, and selecting on gross gave up 0.332 to 0.615 net Sharpe against selecting on net. That magnitude does not depend on which way the rates are wrong.
  • blockingThe universe is NOT point-in-time. No index-membership source is wired up, so symbols that were not constituents on a given date cannot be detected and delisted names may be silently absent. A survivorship-biased universe inflates returns.
  • blocking5 of the leakage checks in BUILD.md section 5 could not run and were not evaluated: point-in-time constituency, universe rebalance timing, delisting terminal values, corporate-action confirmation, and NaN forward-fill detection. A clean leakage result is only as strong as this list is short.
  • statedRisk-free rate assumed to be zero; NULL has no risk-free series. Beta is unaffected; alpha is shifted by (1 - beta) times the true rate.
  • blockingThe golden fixture suite is not complete. Until all eight fixtures in BUILD.md section 8 return their expected verdicts, a REJECT from NULL may be describing the harness rather than the strategy, and the two cannot be told apart from this report.
