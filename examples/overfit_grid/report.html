Why it failed

beats_benchmark_netNo alpha over buy-and-hold after costs. Strategy returned 12.89% CAGR net of everything against -20.42% for the benchmark, beta 0.12. Annualised alpha 0.40% with a t-stat of 0.31 (Newey-West, 6 lags) over 504 observations; threshold is 2.0, and alpha with a t-stat below 2 is not alpha.

deflated_sharpeThe probability the true Sharpe exceeds zero, after adjusting for how many variants were tried and for the shape of the return distribution, is 0.37 against a threshold of 0.95. Adjusted for selection, this is a search finding noise.

reality_checkBootstrapping the null that the best candidate does not beat the benchmark, using a stationary bootstrap so autocorrelation survives resampling, gives p = 0.095 against a threshold of 0.050. The outperformance is within what chance produces on this history.

sensitivity_plateauThe mean Sharpe of the immediate parameter neighbourhood is 23% of the peak (0.19 against 0.82), threshold 60%. A spike is curve-fitting: the result depends on the exact parameters chosen, and neighbouring values do not work.

capacityNot computable: Evidence carries no per-order ADV participation, so the share of daily traded volume this strategy would consume cannot be checked. Order-level participation data is required. This gate did not judge the strategy either way. It does not count as a pass -- a strategy cannot clear a test that was never run -- but it is reported separately from a genuine failure so the difference stays visible.

selection diagnosticWith 5,000 trials over 504 observations, noise alone is expected to produce a maximum Sharpe of 1.06; the candidate showed 0.82, so the search cannot distinguish this edge from luck.

Evidence panels

These are reported for context and do not vote on the verdict.

mtrlAn annualised Sharpe of 0.82, with skew -0.11 and kurtosis 3.37, would need 4.0 years of observations before it could be called significant at 95% confidence. The backtest is 2.0 years long, so it is under-powered by construction: this Sharpe would need a longer track record than exists before it could be called significant, and no analysis of the existing data changes that.
pboAcross 12,870 symmetric train/test partitions of 1,000 variants, the in-sample best landed below the out-of-sample median 77% of the time (median relative rank 0.26). Under no selection skill every candidate is exchangeable and PBO's expected value is exactly 0.5, so the point estimate alone proves nothing; a moving-block subsampling interval over 15 moving-block subsamples of the underlying series gives [0.76, 1.00] at 95% confidence, and the interval reaches 1.00, at or above the 0.5 null. This is reported as evidence only and does not vote on the verdict. Each replicate used a random 512 of the 12,870 partitions to keep the cost tractable; the point estimate uses all of them. A low PBO is never evidence that an edge is real. With many candidates over a short sample, the maximum of N noise Sharpes routinely exceeds a genuine edge's own Sharpe, so the real strategy is not the one selected and PBO ends up describing a search that never found it.

Stated limitations — all of them

  • blockingCharge rates have NOT been reconciled against a live broker charge list (configs/costs_india_equity.yaml carries _verified_on: UNVERIFIED). Every cost figure on this report is indicative only, and rates wrong in the optimistic direction inflate every result shown here.
  • blockingThe benchmark series is supplied by the caller and NULL does not select it. It has not been confirmed as a total-return index. If it is a price index, roughly 1.2-1.5%/yr of dividends are missing from the benchmark and every alpha figure here is overstated by that much.
  • blockingThe universe is NOT point-in-time. No index-membership source is wired up, so symbols that were not constituents on a given date cannot be detected and delisted names may be silently absent. A survivorship-biased universe inflates returns.
  • blocking5 of the leakage checks in BUILD.md section 5 could not run and were not evaluated: point-in-time constituency, universe rebalance timing, delisting terminal values, corporate-action confirmation, and NaN forward-fill detection. A clean leakage result is only as strong as this list is short.
  • statedRisk-free rate assumed to be zero; NULL has no risk-free series. Beta is unaffected; alpha is shifted by (1 - beta) times the true rate.
  • blocking1 gate(s) could not be evaluated at all and did not judge this strategy either way: capacity. They are counted as failures, not passes, but the strategy has not actually been tested against them.
  • blockingThe golden fixture suite is not complete. Until all eight fixtures in BUILD.md section 8 return their expected verdicts, a REJECT from NULL may be describing the harness rather than the strategy, and the two cannot be told apart from this report.
